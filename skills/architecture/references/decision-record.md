# Decision record template

If the project has no format of its own, use this template. Fill every section. Delete the guidance lines in parentheses.

```markdown
# <Decision as a short statement>

Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
Status: <proposed | accepted | replaced by ...>

## Question

(The decision as one question, and what makes it urgent now.)

## Forces

- Quality attributes, ranked: (1., 2., 3.)
- Hard constraints: (...)
- Soft constraints: (...)
- Assumptions: (...)

## Options

### <Option name>

(One paragraph. What it makes easy, what it makes hard, how expensive it is to reverse.)

## Decision

(The chosen option and the deciding tradeoff: "A buys X at the cost of Y.")

## Accepted costs

- (What becomes harder, and who pays for it.)

## Fitness functions

- (Attribute: what the check measures, where it runs, and its threshold.)

## Review when

- (The event or measurement that triggers a new look at this decision.)
```
